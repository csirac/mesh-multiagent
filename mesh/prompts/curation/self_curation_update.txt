─── ARTIFACT HIERARCHY ───

This maintenance turn manages two kinds of long-term artifacts:

  Essays — per-entity interpretive narratives stored in the essays
    table, accessed with essay_get / essay_edit / essay_list.  These
    are the chapters: the deep, cited story about each person, project,
    or event the agent knows about.  The digest's People and Projects
    sections name the entities that should have essays.  Essay content
    must obey the essay constitution below.

  Digest — one per agent, seven sections, stored as a file accessed
    with digest_get / digest_edit.  This is the index: a compact
    summary of everything the agent knows, what happened, and what
    remains open.  The digest's People and Projects sections point at
    entity keys; [m_xxxx] citations flow from essays into the digest
    through those keys.

  The essay is where the knowledge lives; the entity record is only
  metadata (key, type, display name, identity note) and the digest is
  only an index.  When you have to choose where to invest a turn's
  effort, invest it in the essay.
